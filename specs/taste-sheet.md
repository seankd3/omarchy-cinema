# Taste sheet

Questions for Sean, one line each, with stills and a recommendation, and the
coordinator's rulings he has not overridden (each built as said; the full
wording and numbers are in git history).

1. Gaps scale with the screen: 64 px per 1440 of the smallest screen's
   shorter side (48 at 1080). Stills: scratch/batch2/gaps/.
2. The world's view does not move with the pointer, in any film; a dragged
   window still pulls it. Stills: scratch/batch2/view/.
3. The Matrix's far rain goes soft and its near rain stays off the glass.
   Stills: scratch/batch2/depth/.
4. The plugin's frost: a little brighter and sharper than Hyprland's was;
   muted text holds 4.5:1. Stills: scratch/batch2/frame-budget/.
5. A window's rim shows the world at half size: Tron's and the Matrix's
   bent glints turn to soft dots, 0.3 ms a frame. Stills: scratch/batch3/taste/rim-*.
6. Notifications show the film's frost through their cards, 0.8 ms a frame.
   Stills: scratch/batch3/taste/notify-*.
7. The films' own motion holds still at rest and moves while windows do
   (specs/smoothness.md, "Still at rest").
8. No light round the pointer: a mouse move drew the whole world (4.07 ms a
   frame in Jarvis, now 0.18). Stills: scratch/batch6/taste/pointer/.
